Browsers do not put coordinates in HTTP. Precise location always asks. We skip the ask.

The server reads Vercel geo headers, or a coarse IP lookup in development, then Open-Meteo. VPN cities are expected. Atmosphere still works.

This is a stand-in article for the climate layer on this site.
